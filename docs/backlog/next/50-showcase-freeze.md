## Why
Feature work after the tag makes the tag meaningless. A freeze is what turns a demo into an artifact.

## In scope
- Two weeks of oracle-bug-only changes after the tag
- `attic:` issue template for new ideas

## Out of scope
- Stopping bug fixes. Oracle bugs are always in scope.

## Acceptance
- [ ] PLAN-90 status says freeze
- [ ] The changelog shows no feature PRs during the window

Nothing distinguished a bug fix from a feature during a freeze.
