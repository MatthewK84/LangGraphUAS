Freefly Astro - battery pack limitations (extract)

Operating temperature. Astro can operate between -20 and 50 C. Performance and
endurance degrade at both ends of that range, and the figures published for
endurance assume conditions near the middle of it.

Battery temperature at takeoff. Batteries should be kept warm, above 10 C at
takeoff. Once flying, self-heating keeps them warm. This is a limit on the pack
and its procedure, not on the airframe: the aircraft tolerates far colder air
than the pack tolerates being launched at.

Cold behaviour. If batteries become too cold, state of charge will decline
quickly and the aircraft will enter Return or Land mode. A capacity derate
applied in planning is not a substitute for warming the pack before flight.
