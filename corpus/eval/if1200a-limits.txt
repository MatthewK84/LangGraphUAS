Inspired Flight IF1200A - operating limits (SYNTHETIC EVAL DOCUMENT, NOT A PUBLICATION)

Operating temperature. The airframe is rated from -20 C to 50 C.

Maximum wind. Sustained wind resistance is published as 12 m/s.

Battery capacity. The dual-pack configuration carries 1332 Wh total.

Maximum takeoff mass. Maximum gross takeoff mass is 15.9 kg including payload.

Payload capacity. The airframe carries up to 4.5 kg of payload within the gross
mass limit above.
