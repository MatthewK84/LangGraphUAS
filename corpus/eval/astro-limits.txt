Freefly Astro - operating limits (SYNTHETIC EVAL DOCUMENT, NOT A PUBLICATION)

Operating temperature. The airframe is rated from -20 C to 50 C. Endurance
figures published elsewhere assume conditions near the middle of that band and
degrade at both ends.

Battery temperature at takeoff. Do not launch with pack temperature below 10 C.
Once airborne, self-heating sustains the pack. This is a limit on the pack and
its procedure, not on the airframe, and the two are frequently confused.

Maximum wind. Sustained wind of 12 m/s is the published limit for the airframe.
Gusts beyond that figure are outside the tested envelope.

Battery capacity. The standard pack is rated 274 Wh. Usable capacity below that
figure should be assumed whenever the pack is cold.

Maximum takeoff mass. The airframe is rated to 6.8 kg including payload.
