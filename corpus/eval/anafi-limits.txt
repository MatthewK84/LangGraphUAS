Parrot ANAFI USA - operating limits (SYNTHETIC EVAL DOCUMENT, NOT A PUBLICATION)

Operating temperature. The aircraft operates from -10 C to 43 C. The upper
figure is the conservative one carried in the reference pack.

Maximum wind. The published resistance figure is 14.7 m/s for level flight.

Battery capacity. The smart battery is rated 3400 mAh at 11.55 V, which is
approximately 39 Wh.

Maximum takeoff mass. Total mass is 500 g with no external payload fitted.

Flight time. Published endurance is 32 minutes under the manufacturer's test
profile, which is not a mission profile.
